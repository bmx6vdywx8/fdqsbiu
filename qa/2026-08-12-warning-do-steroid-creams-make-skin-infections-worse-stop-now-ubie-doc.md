Steroid creams, also known as topical corticosteroids, are often used to reduce inflammation and itching. However, if they are applied to a skin infection, they can actually make the infection worse. This happens because steroid creams can suppress the body's natural immune response, making it easier for the infection to spread and harder for the body to fight it off.

Key Points:

Infections Can Spread: Using steroid creams on infections can hide the redness and swelling, which are signs of infection. This can make the infection seem better when it is not.

Body's Defense is Lowered: Steroid creams can weaken the skin's ability to fight germs, allowing infections to grow.
