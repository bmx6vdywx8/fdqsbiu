Explanation

Lupus is a condition that affects the immune system. It is important to know that lupus is not contagious, so you cannot catch it from someone else. However, there is a possibility that lupus can run in families, which means it might be hereditary. This suggests that if someone in your family has lupus, you might have a higher chance of having it too.

Understanding lupus can be challenging, and people may have different ideas about it. Some families might not know much about lupus, especially in certain parts of the world.
