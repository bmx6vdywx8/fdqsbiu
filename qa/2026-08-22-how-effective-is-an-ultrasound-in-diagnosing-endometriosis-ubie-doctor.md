Explanation

Ultrasound is a tool doctors use to look inside the body. It can help find endometriosis, which is when tissue like the lining of the uterus grows outside it. Ultrasound works well for some types of endometriosis, like when it grows on the ovaries or forms large cysts. However, it may not see smaller areas of endometriosis or those in tricky spots.
