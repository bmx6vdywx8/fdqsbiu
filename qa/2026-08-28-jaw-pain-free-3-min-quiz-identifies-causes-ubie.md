About the Symptom

[Pain Free](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/blog/3942206869.html)

Jaw pain is referring to painful sensation or tenderness that is felt in or around the jaw. This may be from the jaw itself including the nearby temporomandibular joint (TMJ) or due to surrounding structures including muscles, nerves, lymph nodes or even due to masses. Causes can include trauma, infection, inflammation, or abnormal growths.
