Explanation

White lines on the nails, also known as leukonychia, can have different causes. They might appear as horizontal or vertical lines and can be full or partial. These lines can result from minor injuries to the nail, infections, or even systemic health issues.

Types of Leukonychia

Total Leukonychia: The entire nail appears white.

Partial Leukonychia: Only parts of the nail are white.

Transverse Leukonychia: Horizontal white lines across the nail.

Possible Causes

Injury: Small injuries to the nail can cause white spots or lines.

Infections: Certain infections can lead to changes in nail color.
