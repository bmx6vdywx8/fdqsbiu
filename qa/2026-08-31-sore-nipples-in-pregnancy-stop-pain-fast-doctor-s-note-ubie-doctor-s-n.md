Nipple soreness during pregnancy is a common early symptom, typically caused by rising hormone levels (estrogen and progesterone), increased blood flow to the breasts, and rapid breast tissue growth as your body prepares for breastfeeding. Most women experience tenderness between weeks 4 and 7 of pregnancy, though it can occur anytime.

Common causes of sore nipples in pregnancy:

Hormonal shifts (estrogen, progesterone, prolactin)

Increased breast size and sensitivity

Milk duct development

Friction from bras or clothing

Ways to ease nipple soreness:

Wear a soft, supportive maternity bra

Apply lanolin cream or pure coconut oil

Use warm (not hot) compresses

Avoid harsh soaps on the nipple area

Let nipples air-dry after bathing

While sore nipples are usually harmless, they can occasionally signal an infection, skin condition, or other concern that deserves attention.
