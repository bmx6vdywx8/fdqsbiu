Is consuming rice beneficial for someone with diarrhea?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Rice is a recommended food for people with diarrhea because it is easy to digest, low in fiber, and helps firm up stools. As a bland, starchy carbohydrate, white rice slows bowel movements, replenishes lost energy, and improves nutrient absorption during recovery.

If diarrhea is disrupting your day or lingering longer than expected, it's worth understanding why. Diet tweaks like rice can ease symptoms, but the root cause matters just as much as the relief.
