What are the potential causes of low energy levels in males, and how might testosterone play a role?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Low energy in males can be caused by many factors, including low testosterone levels. Testosterone is a hormone that helps keep energy up, and when levels drop, it may lead to feeling tired, weak, or unmotivated.

Explanation

There are several reasons a man might feel low energy. Sometimes, it can be from not getting enough sleep, poor nutrition, stress, or lack of exercise. Other reasons include medical conditions such as thyroid problems, sleep apnea, or depression. Testosterone is a hormone that plays a big role in keeping our bodies active and strong.

When a man has low testosterone—a condition called male hypogonadism—it can make him feel even more tired. This hormone helps boost our energy, muscle strength, and mood. When testosterone levels drop naturally with age or because of other issues, it can lead to fatigue and low energy.

Research and reviews from trusted medical sources explain that low testosterone can affect many parts of the body, which is why some men experience tiredness as one of the symptoms.
