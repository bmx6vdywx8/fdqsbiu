Explanation

Hair loss can be influenced by various factors, and making some healthy lifestyle changes may be necessary.

Nutrition: Having a balanced diet rich in vitamins and minerals is important. Vitamins like A, C, D, E, and minerals like zinc and iron play a role in maintaining healthy hair. Making sure your diet includes these nutrients can help reduce hair loss.

Overall Health: Maintaining good overall health by managing stress and avoiding harmful hair treatments can also be beneficial.
