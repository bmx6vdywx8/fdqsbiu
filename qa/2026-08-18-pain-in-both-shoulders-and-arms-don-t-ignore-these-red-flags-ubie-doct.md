Pain in both shoulders and arms can have several causes. One common reason is an issue with the neck, which can affect how you feel in your shoulders and arms. Problems with the cervical spine, which is part of your neck, can lead to pain in these areas.

Sometimes, nerves in the neck can get irritated or pinched, and this can make your shoulders and arms hurt. Often pinched nerves in the cervical spine are related to bulging discs between the vertebrae (bones in the cervical spine).
