Is it common for nipples to become more sensitive during my period?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, nipple sensitivity during your period is common and typically caused by hormonal changes. Fluctuations in estrogen and progesterone in the days leading up to and during menstruation can increase blood flow to breast tissue, making nipples feel tender, sore, or more reactive to touch. This symptom often resolves once your period ends and hormone levels stabilize.

However, persistent, severe, or unusual nipple sensitivity isn't always linked to your cycle. It can also signal other conditions such as hormonal imbalances, skin irritation, infection, or, in rare cases, underlying health concerns worth discussing with a clinician.
