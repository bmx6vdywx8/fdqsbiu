Ramelteon and melatonin are both used to support sleep, but they differ in important ways. Melatonin is an over-the-counter supplement that mimics the body's natural sleep hormone, while ramelteon is a prescription medication that selectively targets melatonin receptors (MT1 and MT2) in the brain. Ramelteon may offer specific benefits for people with REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD), a condition where individuals physically act out their dreams during sleep.

If you're experiencing sleep disturbances, unusual nighttime movements, or vivid dream enactment, it's important to identify the underlying cause before choosing a treatment. Symptoms of RBD can overlap with other sleep and neurological conditions, so self-diagnosis can be risky. Ramelteon is a prescription medication that works by targeting specific receptors in the brain to help regulate the sleep-wake cycle. It has been shown to have beneficial effects on REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD) by potentially reducing the frequency of disruptive sleep behaviors.
