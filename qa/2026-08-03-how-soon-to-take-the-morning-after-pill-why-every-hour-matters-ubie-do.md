How soon should I take the morning-after pill for it to work?

Internal Medicine

The morning-after pill is most effective when taken as soon as possible after unprotected sex. For best results, take it within 72 hours (3 days), though some types may work up to 120 hours (5 days) later. The sooner it's taken, the higher the chance of preventing pregnancy.
