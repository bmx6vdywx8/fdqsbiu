What does a Lyme disease rash look like?

The Lyme disease rash, known as erythema migrans, is typically a red, circular or oval patch that gradually expands over days. It often forms a distinctive bull's-eye pattern, with a clear center surrounded by a red ring, though not all rashes appear this way. The rash usually appears within 3 to 30 days after a tick bite, is generally painless, and may feel warm to the touch. Early identification is critical, as Lyme disease can lead to serious complications if left untreated.
