How can I identify if my tonsils are swollen?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Swollen tonsils often cause noticeable symptoms, including a sore throat, difficulty swallowing, snoring, and sometimes a muffled voice or bad breath. These signs may indicate that your tonsils are enlarged due to infection, inflammation, or other underlying conditions. Because swollen tonsils can stem from many causes—ranging from viral infections to strep throat or chronic tonsillitis—identifying the root cause matters. Signs of Swollen Tonsils:

Sore Throat: One of the most common signs is a persistent sore throat.

Difficulty Swallowing: Enlarged tonsils can make swallowing uncomfortable or painful.

If you experience these symptoms, it may indicate that your tonsils are swollen.
