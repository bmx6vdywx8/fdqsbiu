Nipple discharge when squeezed is often harmless, especially if it's clear, white, or occurs in both breasts. However, discharge that is bloody, spontaneous, one-sided, or persistent may signal an underlying issue such as a blocked duct, infection, hormonal imbalance, or in rare cases, a breast tumor. Color, consistency, and frequency all offer important clues.

Nipple discharge can occur for various reasons, and in many cases, it is not a cause for concern. However, it is important to pay attention to the type of discharge.

Normal Discharge: Sometimes, squeezing the nipple can cause a small amount of discharge. This is often normal and can happen due to hormonal changes or physical stimulation.
