A pulling sensation in the legs at night is often caused by muscle cramps, nerve issues such as sciatica or restless legs syndrome, joint disorders, or circulation problems. Common triggers include dehydration, mineral imbalances (like low potassium or magnesium), prolonged sitting, pregnancy, or underlying conditions affecting the nerves or blood vessels.

Because nighttime leg symptoms can stem from many different causes—some minor, others requiring medical attention—it's important to identify what's driving your discomfort before it disrupts more of your sleep or worsens over time.
