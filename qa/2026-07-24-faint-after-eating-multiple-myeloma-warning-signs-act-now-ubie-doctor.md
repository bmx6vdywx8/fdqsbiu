Internal Medicine

Feeling faint after eating in people with multiple myeloma can be caused by postprandial hypotension, a temporary drop in blood pressure that occurs after meals as blood flow is redirected to the digestive system. Multiple myeloma may also contribute to this symptom through cardiac amyloidosis, anemia, or dehydration, all of which can affect circulation and heart function. These factors reduce the blood supply reaching the brain, leading to lightheadedness or dizziness after eating.

Because faintness after eating can signal several overlapping conditions, understanding the underlying cause is essential to getting the right care.
