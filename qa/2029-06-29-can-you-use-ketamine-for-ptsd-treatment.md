What is ketamine, and how does it combat PTSD?

Ketamine is a medication used for treating mental health issues, including PTSD. It is a fast-acting anesthetic used for years in medical settings to produce anesthesia. Ketamine blocks the brain’s glutamate receptors, which are responsible for the fear and anxiety associated with PTSD. In addition, it can help regulate emotions, reduce depression and improve cognitive functioning.

It can be administered as an intravenous infusion, intramuscular injection, or nasal spray. Ketamine infusions deliver small doses of the drug directly into the bloodstream. The dosage and frequency are adjusted based on the individual’s symptoms and response. During the infusion, individuals can experience a brief dissociation and sedation. Afterward, they often feel a sense of well-being and relief from their PTSD symptoms.

These methods teach individuals how to cope better with their symptoms and make positive life changes.
