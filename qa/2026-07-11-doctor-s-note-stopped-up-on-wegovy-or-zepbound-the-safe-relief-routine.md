Doctor's Note: Stopped Up on Wegovy or Zepbound? The Safe Relief Routine Every Sister Needs

Are you a Black woman taking a GLP-1 medication like Wegovy® or Zepbound® and finding yourself… well, stopped up? You're not alone. Constipation is one of the most common side effects of GLP-1 agonists, affecting up to 40% of people on these medications. The good news: with a few simple changes and safe remedies, you can stay regular—without anxiety or harsh products.

Why GLP-1 Medications Can Cause Constipation

GLP-1 agonists work by slowing stomach emptying and reducing appetite. That slowdown often extends to your intestines, leading to harder, less frequent stools. Add typical diet and lifestyle factors—and if you're a Black woman, cultural food preferences or busy schedules—you might find relief harder to come by.

Key contributors:

Slowed gastrointestinal motility

Low dietary fiber

Inadequate fluid intake

Limited physical activity

Stress or travel disrupting routine

The Safe Relief Routine Every Sister Needs

This four-part plan draws on evidence-based approaches and cultural considerations to help you stay regular.

1. Optimize Your Diet

• Increase fiber gradually (aim for 25–30 g/day) – Whole grains: oats, brown rice, quinoa – Fruits and veggies: berries, pears, leafy greens, okra – Legumes: black-eyed peas, kidney beans• Embrace culturally familiar high-fiber foods – Collard greens, sweet potatoes, plantains• Limit constipating foods – Too much cheese, processed foods, white rice or flour• Spread fiber throughout the day – Breakfast: oatmeal with berries – Snacks: apple slices with nut butter – Dinner: lentil stew with leafy greens

2. Hydrate Smartly

• Aim for 8–10 cups (64–80 oz) of fluid daily• Water is best—add slices of lemon or cucumber for flavor• Herbal teas and brothy soups count too• Avoid excessive caffeine or alcohol, which can dehydrate

• Regular movement stimulates your gut• Aim for at least 30 minutes most days: – Brisk walks, dancing to your favorite playlist, yoga• Simple core exercises can help: – Pelvic tilts, knee-to-chest stretches• Even 5–10 minutes after a meal can kickstart digestion

4. Safe, Over-the-Counter Support

If diet, fluids, and movement aren't enough after one to two weeks, consider gentle OTC remedies. Always follow package instructions and check with your provider.

Final Thoughts

Constipation on Wegovy or Zepbound doesn't have to derail your health journey. With a balanced diet, enough fluids, regular movement, and safe use of OTC aids, you can find relief and keep your bowels moving smoothly.

Always speak to a doctor before starting any new supplement or if you suspect something serious. If at any point you feel your symptoms are life threatening—severe pain, blood in vomit or stool, signs of dehydration—seek emergency care immediately.
