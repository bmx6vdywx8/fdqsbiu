Hi J.R.,

Thanks for your question. Most men who want a full beard would like to look a little more rugged, but you have to be careful not to venture into werewolf territory. We recommend regular trimming while growing it out to keep your look neat. But remember a little can go a long way — don’t cut everything off. Avoid harsh lines that will draw attention to your face — the bad kind.

Allan Peterkin, author of One Thousand Beards: A Cultural History of Facial Hair, defines the beard line as about halfway between your Adam’s apple and your chin. So fade the edges of your beard with a pair of trimmers with guards like the Panasonic All-in-One Cordless Beard Trimmer ($40, panasonic.com) and finish off your look with a light-hold pomade to keep those stubborn hairs in place. Don’t neglect the cheeks, as well. If hair creeps up towards your cheekbone, use the same razor to let your skin show.
