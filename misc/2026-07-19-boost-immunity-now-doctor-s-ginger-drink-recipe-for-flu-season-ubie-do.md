Ginger Tea Recipe

Ingredients:

1-inch piece of fresh ginger, peeled and sliced

1 lemon, juiced

1-2 tablespoons of honey

2 cups of water

Instructions:

Place the sliced ginger in a small pot with 2 cups of water.

Bring the water to a boil, then reduce the heat and let it simmer for about 10 minutes.

Remove from heat and strain the liquid into a cup.

Add the lemon juice and honey, stirring until the honey is dissolved.
