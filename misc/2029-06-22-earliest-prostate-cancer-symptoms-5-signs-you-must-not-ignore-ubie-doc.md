# Earliest Prostate Cancer Symptoms: 5 Signs You Must Not Ignore | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

What are the earliest symptoms of prostate cancer?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

[Ubie Doctor's](https://yldzdh.com/vj1clgyx/)

Internal Medicine

The earliest symptoms of prostate cancer often involve changes in urination. These may include:

Difficulty starting urination

A weak or interrupted urine stream

Frequent urination, especially at night (nocturia)

A sudden or urgent need to urinate

Trouble fully emptying the bladder

In some cases, early prostate cancer causes no symptoms at all, which is why awareness of subtle changes is important. Less common early signs can include blood in the urine or semen, and discomfort in the pelvic area.

Because these symptoms often overlap with non-cancerous conditions like an enlarged prostate (BPH) or a urinary tract infection, they can be difficult to interpret on your own. Getting clarity early matters — the sooner you understand what may be causing your symptoms, the sooner you can take informed next steps with a healthcare provider.
