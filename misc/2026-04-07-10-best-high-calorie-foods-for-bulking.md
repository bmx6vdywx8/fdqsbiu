Best High Calorie Foods For Bulking

Bulking is all about increasing your calorie intake and monitoring your progress to keep the weight gain in check while maximizing muscle growth. Here are some of the best calorie-rich foods for bulking:

Protein and Healthy Fats

Fats are the food group that has the highest calories per gram. Only 3 tablespoons of oil, or 40 grams have 360 calories compared to 100 grams of whole pork loin which has 262 calories. But, this doesn’t mean you should be downing oil and butter.

Fats like butter and oil, although high-calorie, also have high quantities of saturated fats and monounsaturated fats that have been shown to impact heart health and contribute to high cholesterol levels negatively.

It’s a better idea to swap them for foods with a healthy fat content like eggs, avocados, nut butter nuts, and fish oil.

[Best High Calorie](https://focus-and-calm.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Protein-rich foods are made of essential amino acids that are necessary for muscle mass and muscle recovery. Meats like chicken, beef, and pork are high in calories and protein.

You should eat 1.4-2 grams of protein for every kg of body weight per day, in combination with exercise, to gain muscle mass.

Complex Carbohydrates

Complex carbohydrates are the best for muscle recovery so they’re a popular bulking food. Foods like rice, oats, and sweet potatoes offer a high-calorie count, along with the slow-releasing energy needed to power through intense workouts.

These carbs not only help replenish muscle glycogen stores after exercise but also ensure that your body has the fuel it needs to perform at its best.

By balancing your intake of protein, complex carbohydrates, and fats, you can create a bulking diet that supports both muscle gain and overall well-being.

The only downfall is that carbs can make you feel full very quickly so try to pace yourself and have them with every meal.

What is the difference between clean bulk and dirty bulk foods?

When talking about the best high calorie foods for bulking, dirty bulking and eating junk food does not fit the bill. As junk food will only give you fat gain and not much strength or bulking.

Clean bulking focuses on nutrient-dense, whole foods like lean meats, veggies, and whole grains to build muscle while staying healthy. You can minimize unwanted weight gain by focusing on a clean bulk.

Dirty bulking, on the other hand, means eating more calorie-dense, less nutritious foods, like fast food and sugary snacks, to gain weight quickly.

How many calories should I consume for bulking?

Generally, a caloric surplus of 250-500 calories per day above your maintenance level is recommended for muscle gain.

Can I eat junk food while bulking?

While occasional treats can be part of healthy bulking, it’s important to prioritize nutrient-dense foods and a high-protein diet because it ensures that you’re getting the vitamins and minerals necessary for overall health benefits and muscle growth, rather than just empty calories.

How can I increase my calorie intake without feeling overly full?

To increase your calorie intake without feeling overly full, focus on calorie-dense foods like nuts, seeds, and avocados. Incorporate liquid calories through smoothies and protein shakes, and eat smaller, more frequent meals throughout the day.

Are all saturated fats bad for you?

Not all saturated fats are equally harmful to heart health, but excessive saturated fat intake can contribute to high cholesterol levels in your blood, which may raise your risk of heart disease. It’s important to balance your intake and focus on sources of healthy fats.
