What to Look for

"Storage for sex toys protects the investment you make in them," says Javay Frye-Nekrasova, Lovehoney's sex educator. In other words, you want to make sure that the right storage unit is optimized for your most intimate gadgets.

Size

"Before investing in sex toy storage, measure your entire sex toy collection to be sure your box or bag will be able to hold it," says Alvarez. For bigger sex toys like dildos or pegging toys, you may want to find boxes that are at least 10 inches wide.

Materials

For a home storage unit, you want a shell that's made from ultra-durable materials like hard plastic or metal. On the inside, says Frye-Nekrasova, "You'll want softer materials, but nothing that will dirty up or corrode your toys." That means avoiding silicone linings, since that can rub up against your silicone toys and erode.

Design

If you're looking for a discreet storage, then look for an opaque shell (think: leather pouches that also look great).

"Also look for removable or adjustable trays to allow for toys of different sizes and shapes, antimicrobial interior coatings, and USB ports for charging toys when not in use," says Alvarez. "Some might find it useful to have a lock in order to avoid any unwanted breaches."

But if you're looking for a storage unit that's easy to access, Taylor recommends see-through bags, so it's easy to keep track of what's inside.
