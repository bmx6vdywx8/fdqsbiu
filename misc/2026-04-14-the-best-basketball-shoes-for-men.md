# The Best Basketball Shoes For Men

Now that the NBA (and your office pick-up league) is back, there’s no better time to find new kicks.

The Men’s Health Style Team combed through the sneaker universe to pick the best of the best. Some are geared more toward on-court performance, while others exist for pure flash off the hardwood.

[The Best Basketball Shoes](https://satietybrief.china-akan.com/blog/3062750726.html)

Read on to find the perfect pair for you.

UA Curry 3

Athlete/Brand: Steph Curry, Under Armour

Your On-Court Style: These shoes are perfect for players who are flashy, confident, and full of finesse—like Curry himself.

Your Off-Court Style: Also like Curry, your killer instinct is only beset by your unfortunate fragility.

After the debacle of the Curry 2 series—which looked like sneakers your dad would wear—the Golden State star and Under Armour have bounced back with a flashier, more stylish shoe.

The colors are bold, the look is streamlined, and the tiny, Curry-specific flourishes—the MVP’s signature, the “All Things” inscription—bring a new sense of pride to the once-shaky line. (See Why Steph Curry Is the Best Player in the NBA.)

Don these with confidence. It’s time for a comeback year.

($140, underarmour.com)

Nike LeBron Soldier 10

Athlete/Brand: LeBron James, Nike

Your Off-Court Style: You can be petty at times, but there’s no doubt you’re a born leader and a unifying locker room presence.

[Shoes For](https://memorycircuits.hbsnzj.com/)

[The Best](https://benpepsi.com/blog/6357183645.html)

Nike Kobe 11 Low

Athlete/Brand: Kobe Bryant, Nike

Your On-Court Style: People respect you, but—let’s be real—they probably don’t like playing with you.

Your Off-Court Style: Even if it takes you 50 attempts, you’re going to do whatever you want in life.

And if anyone tells you it’s too much, remember: Too much is a matter of perspective. Some people thought Mozart had too many notes in his compositions.*

Adidas Crazylight Boost 2.5 Low

Athlete/Brand: James Harden, Adidas

Your On-Court Style: You don’t exactly hustle back on defense.

Your Off-Court Style: Excessive—particularly in the realms of facial hair and associations with the Kardashian family.

You’re a low-top purist, but you want something a bit roomier than what Nike has to offer. Enter Harden’s line from Adidas.

There aren’t a lot of color choices outside of the Houston Rockets’ red and white, but if you want something a bit more versatile than classic-looking hoop shoes, these can pass off the court just fine.

($130, adidas.com)

Reebok Question Mid Practice

Athlete/Brand: Allen Iverson, Reebok

Your On-Court Style: Your trash talk could bring a man to tears.

Your Off-Court Style: Simply one of a kind.

The mark of an original shoe: Low odds you’ll run into anyone else rocking the same pair.

The Practice has numerous callbacks to AI’s famous “talkin’ about practice” speech in 2002: The baseball design is reminiscent of the cap he wore during the press conference, and the in-sole is marked with choice lines and quotes from the speech itself.

It’s a great talking point for collectors, shoe geeks, and casual ballers alike.

($130, reebok.com)

Adidas Crazy Explosive

Athlete/Brand: Nick Young, Adidas

Your On-Court Style: Your tendency to showboat leads to a lot of regretful mistakes, but hey, the crowd loves it.

Your Off-Court Style: Your life is basically Entourage.

The Crazy Explosive line from Adidas carries the name of a few other athletes (most notably Andrew Wiggins), but Nick Young’s all-white editions are the best look.

With tons of cushioning and overlays to secure your foot, these kicks—unlike the name behind them—are focused purely on the game.

(150, eastbay.com)

Converse Chuck II

Athlete/Brand: No athlete, Nike

Your On-Court Style: You’re so classic you don’t even look for the 3-point line.

Your Off-Court Style: You have old-fashioned sensibilities but a nose for new ideas.

Here’s the bottom line: If you want a versatile, timeless look, venture no further than the excellent Converse Chuck IIs.

They’re less clownish than the originals and strike the perfect balance between casual comfort and authoritative style.

With these, you don’t need to go anywhere near a basketball court to call yourself a baller.

($110, nike.com)
