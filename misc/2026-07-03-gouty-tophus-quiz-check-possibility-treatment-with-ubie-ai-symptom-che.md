What is Gouty Tophus?

It is a type of chronic arthritis, usually chronic rheumatoid arthritis that occurs due to excessive production or reduced clearance of uric acid, which accumulates as uric acid crystals in the joints. Risk factors include genetics, excessive alcohol consumption, a diet with excessive sugars or proteins, certain medications, and male sex.

Treatment of Gouty Tophus

Treatment includes adequate hydration, reducing alcohol consumption, medication to prevent uric acid accumulation, and anti-inflammatory medication.
