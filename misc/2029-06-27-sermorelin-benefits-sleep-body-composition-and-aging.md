How Sermorelin Works

Sermorelin is a synthetic GHRH analog that signals the pituitary gland to produce and release growth hormone. The release pattern is effective because it mimics the body’s own natural rhythm. An increase in growth hormone leads to an increase in IGF-1, which drives the mechanisms within your body that promote better vitality and more efficient energy usage. Supplementing with sermorelin is different from taking direct synthetic HGH because you’re instead signaling the body to make its own natural growth hormone. Benefits for Sleep Quality

Better, more consistent sleep is widely reported and clinically supported in studies of sermorelin. It is often one of the first positive changes noted within the first few weeks of taking sermorelin. Research shows that people taking sermorelin experience improvements in the deep, slow-wave sleep stage when the body’s natural GH secretion peaks. Among patients who report positive sleep changes on sermorelin, the benefits include falling asleep faster, waking up less often in the night, and waking up more rested and refreshed in the morning.

At this point, clinicians have decades of research to point to on the benefits of sermorelin for sleep, beginning with work published on the GH-sleep relationship by Dr. Eve Van Cauter at the University of Chicago in the 1990s.

Up to 70% of nightly growth hormone (GH) secretion is activated by the slow-wave (SW) sleep that occurs in our deepest sleep [Van Cauter 1998]. However, additional research finds that the GH secretion surge that comes as a result of sleep decreases with aging. Pharmacological stimulation of SW sleep results in increased GH release, and compounds that increase SW sleep may therefore represent a novel class of GH secretagogues [Van Cauter 2000].

Benefits for Body Composition

Human studies show that sermorelin can aid in fat loss and redistribution [Stanley and Grinspoon 2016]. One thing that is notable is that a significant portion of the fat loss is attributed to visceral and abdominal fats that are associated with poor health outcomes and increased mortality risks. The benefits of sermorelin for belly fat are owed to its ability to trigger GH and IGF-1 that initiate a process of lipolysis that breaks down stored fats to be used for energy.

By facilitating the release of the body’s own growth hormone, sermorelin promotes maintenance of lean tissue and muscle gains in adults with age-related GH decline. Some benefits of sermorelin for muscle building include revived strength, hypertrophy (bigger muscles), and boosted athletic performance.

People taking sermorelin also see improved metabolic markers, such as improvements in insulin sensitivity, lipid profiles, and other signals of metabolic health. According to findings, sermorelin doesn’t trigger an increase in fasting blood glucose and induce insulin resistance as direct HGH injections can, due to the fact that it acts by naturally stimulating the pituitary gland. For patients taking sermorelin, this can result in preserved or improved glucose homeostasis and insulin sensitivity [Jeffcoate 2002].

Benefits for the Aging Process

As we age, we enter a natural transition called somatopause. Beginning at around age 30 for most people, somatopause is the natural and progressive decline of the body’s growth hormone (GH) and insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1) levels. However, rates actually begin declining after puberty before accelerating more rapidly with age. Somatopause results in people over age 60 having just 30% to 50% of their peak hormone levels from youth. By stimulating the pituitary gland, sermorelin can help to restore the body’s natural growth hormone production to reverse many common physical and mental symptoms of hormone-related aging.

How Long Until You See Benefits?

With dosing protocols individualized by patient, timelines for seeing results can vary. In most cases, patients report sleep improvements somewhere between two and four weeks following supplementation. It often takes one to three months for patients to notice a change in energy levels, recovery ability, and mood.

For body composition changes, the timeline is a little fuller because of the changes happening on the biochemical and cellular levels that take time to present physically. This usually means noticing significant changes in appearance and weight between three and six months. Finally, outward and inward benefits tied to overall aging often take six to 12 months of consistent supplementation to become noticeable. This is when most patients can clearly see and feel the benefits tied to skin, bone health, and overall aging. The important thing to remember is that sermorelin results need to be closely monitored under clinical supervision. This allows a care provider and patient to work together to tweak doses and protocols based on lab results.

What the Research Doesn’t Yet Show

Sermorelin success stories are helping people tackle the difficult parts of aging using a gentle, natural, and progressive approach to triggering growth hormone production. While we already have decades of positive research, the full benefits and realities of sermorelin supplementation are really only just starting to fully emerge as researchers have better technology and bigger data sets to work with.

[Benefits Sleep Body](https://allpepay.com/)

As of now, researchers don’t have much long-term safety data going more than a period of several years. That information is still accumulating as sermorelin patients are tracked for longer periods. While sermorelin shows strong evidence for supporting things like skin health, bone health, improved body composition, and better mood, it’s important not to overstate what supplementation is capable of providing. Claims of sermorelin being an anti-aging wonder drug that stops the clock simply aren’t supported. There is also no concrete evidence for now that sermorelin can extend lifespan or reverse disease. What’s more, many of the common benefits attributed to sermorelin are only based on observational reports rather than randomized trials so far.

The takeaway is that evidence of sermorelin helping to fight aging is still very much emerging. What we do know from strong clinical research that has been done so far is that it is very effective at triggering the body’s natural growth hormone production via the pituitary gland. This helps patients who take sermorelin to enjoy many of the “revamping” benefits tied to things like skin thickness and elasticity, visceral fat reduction, and mood improvements.
