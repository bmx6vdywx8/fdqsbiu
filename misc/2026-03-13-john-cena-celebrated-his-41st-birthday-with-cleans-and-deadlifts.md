# John Cena Celebrated His 41st Birthday With Cleans and Deadlifts

John Cena just turned 41 years old, and like many of us would do, he headed to a couple of bars to celebrate. The only difference? Cena's version of a bar has a lot more weight plates.

The newly-single WWE star-turned-actor showed off his birthday lifting session on Twitter, proving that being in your 40s really has little impact on what you can achieve in the gym (for more proof, look at this UFC fighter). In the video above, Cena throws up a 160-kilogram clean. Not too shabby.

"41 years old. 160kg. NEVER let age define effort. #EarnTheDay #NeverGiveUp #RAW," Cena wrote alongside the video.
