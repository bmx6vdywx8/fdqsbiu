Are fever-induced seizures common in adults?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Fever-induced seizures, also known as febrile seizures, are rare in adults and occur most commonly in young children between 6 months and 5 years of age. When seizures do occur alongside a fever in adults, they typically signal an underlying condition—such as an infection, neurological disorder, or metabolic imbalance—rather than a simple febrile response.
