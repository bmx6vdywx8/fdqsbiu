Polysomnography is the gold-standard diagnostic test for REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD). This overnight sleep study records brain waves, muscle activity, eye movements, and breathing to detect the abnormal muscle tone and physical movements that occur during REM sleep in people with RBD.

If you're experiencing symptoms like acting out dreams, sudden limb movements, or vocalizations during sleep, understanding what's happening is the first step toward relief. Early identification of RBD matters because it can be linked to other neurological conditions, and effective treatments are available. Explanation

Polysomnography is a special sleep test that helps doctors see how you sleep. It is very important for finding out if someone has REM Sleep Behavior Disorder (RBD). During this test, doctors look at your sleep patterns and check for unusual movements when you are in the REM part of sleep.

How It Works:

Sleep Monitoring: Polysomnography keeps track of your brain waves, heart rate, breathing, and movements while you sleep.

Detecting Movements: It helps find out if you move a lot during REM sleep, which is a sign of RBD.
