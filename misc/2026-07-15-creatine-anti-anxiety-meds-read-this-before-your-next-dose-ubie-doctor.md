# Creatine & Anti-Anxiety Meds: Read This Before Your Next Dose! | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 5/6/2026

Panic & Power: Is it Safe to Use Creatine with Anti-Anxiety Meds?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Can you take creatine with Xanax? There are no known direct interactions between creatine and anti-anxiety medications like Xanax. However, it's still important to monitor hydration, kidney function, blood pressure, and any changes in energy or cognition when combining them.

To minimize side effects while supporting both physical performance and mood, experts suggest starting with a low maintenance dose (3–5g daily), spacing your supplement and medication times apart, and maintaining a balanced diet.

Explanation

Balancing physical performance goals with mental health needs can feel tricky—especially if you're taking anti-anxiety medications like Xanax (alprazolam) and want to try creatine for workouts or mood support. Below, we break down what you need to know about creatine and Xanax for anxiety, possible interactions, safety tips, and when to seek professional advice.

What Is Creatine?

Creatine is one of the most studied sports supplements. It's a naturally occurring compound that helps produce adenosine triphosphate (ATP), the primary energy "currency" for muscle contractions.

Key points:

Naturally found in red meat, fish and made by your liver and kidneys

Loading phase: 20 g/day split into 4 doses for 5–7 days

Maintenance: 3–5 g/day thereafter

Improved strength, power and muscle mass

Faster recovery between intense efforts

Emerging data on cognitive support, mood elevation

What Is Xanax (Alprazolam)?

Xanax is a benzodiazepine frequently prescribed for anxiety, panic disorder and sometimes insomnia.

How it works:

Enhances GABA, a calming neurotransmitter in the brain

Potential Interactions: Creatine + Xanax

To date, there are no well-documented pharmacological interactions between creatine and benzodiazepines like Xanax. They act on entirely different systems:

Creatine: energy metabolism in muscles and possibly brain cells

Xanax: GABAergic system in the central nervous system

That said, you should still consider:

Kidney function

Both creatine supplementation and benzodiazepine metabolism involve the kidneys.

If you have existing kidney issues, monitor kidney function (e.g., serum creatinine, glomerular filtration rate) with your doctor.

Hydration status

Creatine pulls water into muscle cells; inadequate fluid intake may risk dehydration, muscle cramps or gastrointestinal upset.

Xanax isn't dehydrating but may impair your ability to notice mild dehydration (due to drowsiness or sedation).

Blood pressure and heart rate

Creatine may cause mild water retention and weight gain (1–2 kg), which can temporarily raise blood pressure in sensitive individuals.

Xanax can lower blood pressure in some people. Combined effects are unpredictable without monitoring.

Cognitive and motor effects

Xanax can cause drowsiness, slowed reaction time, coordination issues—factors to consider before heavy lifting or intense training.

Creatine has minimal acute cognitive side effects; some studies suggest it may even enhance mental performance, especially under stress.

Safety Considerations

Before adding creatine to a routine that includes anti-anxiety meds:

Talk to your doctorAlways discuss new supplements with the prescribing physician or a pharmacist. They can review your medical history, kidney function, current medications and dosing schedule.

Start slowIf approved, begin with the maintenance dose of 3 g/day instead of a loading phase. This can reduce the risk of gastrointestinal distress.

Stay hydratedAim for at least 2–3 liters of water per day, more on training days. Spread fluids evenly—don't gulp large volumes at once.

Creatine's Potential Mood Benefits

Emerging research suggests creatine might offer more than muscle perks:

Brain energy metabolismIncreased phosphocreatine reserves in brain cells may help neurons meet energy demands during stress or cognitive tasks.

Supporting mental healthSmall trials show creatine can augment treatment for depression, and animal studies hint at reduced anxiety-like behaviors. However, data on anxiety disorders remain preliminary.

Synergy with other treatmentsSome clinicians believe creatine could complement standard therapies (medications, therapy) by bolstering overall brain health. Always view this as an adjunct, not a replacement.

Practical Tips for "Creatine and Xanax for Anxiety"

Plan your supplement schedule

Take creatine with a meal or protein shake to improve absorption and reduce stomach upset.

Space creatine and Xanax doses if possible (e.g., creatine in the morning, Xanax later) to help you notice any new side effects.

Optimize your diet

A balanced intake of lean proteins, healthy fats and whole grains supports both physical and mental health.

When to Seek Medical Help

Creatine is generally safe for healthy adults, and no direct drug-drug interactions with Xanax have been reported. Yet, certain red-flag symptoms warrant prompt evaluation:

Severe abdominal pain, persistent nausea/vomiting

Marked changes in urine output or color

New or worsening chest pain, shortness of breath at rest

Confusion, extreme drowsiness or difficulty waking up
