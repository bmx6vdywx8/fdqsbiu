Explanation

Narcolepsy medications can help manage symptoms but may come with side effects. Sodium oxybate, a common treatment, can cause dizziness and nausea. It is crucial to monitor these side effects and discuss them with a healthcare provider if they become bothersome.

Other Medications:

Samelisant (SUVN-G3031): This medication targets a specific brain receptor and may cause headaches or dizziness.

General Side Effects: Many narcolepsy medications can cause similar side effects, including dizziness, headaches, and nausea.

It's important to keep track of any side effects you experience and talk to your doctor about them. They can help adjust your medication or find other ways to manage the side effects.
