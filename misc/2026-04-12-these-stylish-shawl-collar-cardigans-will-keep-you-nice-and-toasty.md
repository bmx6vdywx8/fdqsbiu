OH, GRANDPA sweater, you say? We don’t care if that designation sticks, shawl collar cardigans are one of the most dashing winter wardrobe essentials out there. Don’t believe us? Just look to style icon Steve McQueen, and more recently, 007 himself, Daniel Craig.

The knitted garment—distinguished from other woolen counterparts by its open front, buttons and, of course, turned-over collar—has endured for two simple reasons: its classic design and its comfiness factor.

Shawl collar cardigans are incredibly versatile, meaning that the best of them can do double duty as tops for both casual and more formal dress codes. Compared to regular cardigans that usually sit closer to the body, the shawl collar cardigan’s baggier silhouette gives off a more relaxed vibe. That said, most still layer wonderfully. Then there’s their material construction, which includes popular fabrics like cashmere and wool that’ll keep you nice and toasty.

So, go on, wrap yourself up and stay fashionably warm this winter with our assortment of the best men’s shawl collar cardigans to buy now.
