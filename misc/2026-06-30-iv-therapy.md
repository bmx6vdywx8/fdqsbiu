IV Therapy Q&A

Am I a good candidate for IV therapy?

Most adults seeking hydration, energy support, immune boosting, or recovery assistance can benefit. Your provider will review your health history to ensure IV therapy is safe and appropriate.

How is IV therapy administered?

IV therapy is delivered through a small IV inserted into your vein, allowing fluids, vitamins, and nutrients to enter your bloodstream quickly. Sessions are comfortable and typically take around 45 minutes.

Are there any risks or side effects?

IV therapy is generally safe when administered by trained medical professionals. Mild side effects can include mild discomfort or temporary bruising at the IV site.
