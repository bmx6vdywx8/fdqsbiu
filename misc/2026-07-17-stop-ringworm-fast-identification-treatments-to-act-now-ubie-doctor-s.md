Explanation

Ringworm is a common skin infection caused by a fungus. It usually appears as a red, circular rash that might be itchy or scaly. The edges of the rash are often raised, while the center might be clear, giving it a ring-like appearance.

Treatment Options:

Creams or lotions applied directly to the skin can help clear up the infection.

For more severe cases, doctors might prescribe oral medications like fluconazole. Studies have shown that fluconazole is effective in treating ringworm of the body (tinea corporis) and groin (tinea cruris). It works by stopping the fungus from growing.

If you're experiencing symptoms that might be ringworm or another skin condition, you can use a free symptom checker to help identify what might be causing your rash. It's important to follow the treatment plan as directed by your healthcare provider to ensure the infection is fully treated and to prevent it from coming back.
