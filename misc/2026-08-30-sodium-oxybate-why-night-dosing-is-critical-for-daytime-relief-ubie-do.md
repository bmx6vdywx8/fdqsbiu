Why is sodium oxybate taken at night to improve daytime symptoms?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Sodium oxybate is taken at night because it works during sleep to improve sleep quality, reduce nighttime awakenings, and help control daytime symptoms of narcolepsy, such as excessive sleepiness and cataplexy.

[Ubie Doctor's](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/)

Because it's a strong central nervous system depressant with a short half-life, sodium oxybate must be taken while lying down in bed, typically in two doses—one at bedtime and another 2.5 to 4 hours later. Taking it at night ensures the medication works when the body naturally rests, promoting deeper, more restorative sleep so patients feel more alert and functional during the day.

Sodium oxybate is a medication used to help people with sleep disorders, like narcolepsy, feel less sleepy during the day. It is taken at night because it works by improving the quality of sleep.

Improved Sleep Quality: According to studies, sodium oxybate helps people get better sleep at night. This means that when they wake up, they feel more rested and alert during the day.
