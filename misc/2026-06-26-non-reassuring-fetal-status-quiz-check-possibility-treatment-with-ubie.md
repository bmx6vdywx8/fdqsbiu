What is Non-Reassuring Fetal Status?

This term describes a suspected lack of oxygen in the fetus during late pregnancy or labor. It can be diagnosed through fetal heart rate monitoring and ultrasound showing decreased fetal movement.

Typical Symptoms of Non-Reassuring Fetal Status

Feel my child is moving less. (Decreased fetal movements)

Currently pregnant

Diagnostic Questions for Non-Reassuring Fetal Status

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Is your child moving less?

Are you pregnant?

Treatment of Non-Reassuring Fetal Status

Urgent specialist care is required. The specific treatment depends on the cause. Hospitalization for further investigation and treatment is often necessary.
