# The Worst Way to Perform the Bulgarian Split Squat

The Bulgarian split squat is a terrific way to build single-leg strength, add lower-body mass, and improve hip mobility—except when it’s done with a long stance.

[to Perform](https://pigmentlab.jcesqc8.com/)

[to Perform the](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/)

[The Worst Way to](https://incretin-path.escortskhi.com/blog/0876112499.html)

When your front foot is too far forward, it causes all sorts of problems, says Men’s Health Fitness Director B.J. Gaddour, who demonstrates the form fail in the video above.

For starters, your lower back must hyperextend—or arch—to keep your chest lifted, he says. This can cause stress on your spine.

[Worst Way to](https://lumina-dermis.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Add load to the movement while in hyperextension, and now you’re setting yourself up for some serious back injuries and pain.

And since your feet are so far apart, you can’t drop very low into the bottom position of the split squat.

“You’re defeating the purpose of the movement, which is to go all the way down and develop full range of motion,” Gaddour explains. The lower you go, the more you can target your quads and glutes.
