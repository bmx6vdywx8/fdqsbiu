How can I get sense of taste and smell back after covid infection?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Most people recover their sense of taste and smell within about one month after a COVID-19 infection. Studies show that more than 80% of individuals experience a full return of these senses within four weeks, though some may take longer to regain them completely.

If you're currently dealing with a loss of taste or smell—or other lingering symptoms—it's worth understanding what could be driving it. Symptoms like these can stem from many causes beyond COVID, including sinus issues, allergies, or other conditions that may need attention.
