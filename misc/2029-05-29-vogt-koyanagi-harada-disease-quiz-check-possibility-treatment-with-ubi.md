What is Vogt-Koyanagi-Harada Disease?

This disease happens when the body's immune system mistakenly attacks a specific type of pigment cells called "melanocytes". The eyes are often affected, causing blurred vision, flashing lights, or floaters. Other areas that can be affected include the inner ear, causing hearing loss. The surface layer of the brain can also be affected, causing headaches and neck stiffness.

Diagnostic Questions for Vogt-Koyanagi-Harada Disease

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Are you experiencing increased hair loss?

Have you experienced partial loss of vision?

Do you have more gray hair than others your age?

Are you experiencing difficulty hearing in one or both ears?

Do you have bloodshot (red) eyes?

Treatment of Vogt-Koyanagi-Harada Disease

This condition is typically treated with strong immune-suppressing medications initially. As the disease becomes controlled, the doctor may change the medication and lower the dose.
