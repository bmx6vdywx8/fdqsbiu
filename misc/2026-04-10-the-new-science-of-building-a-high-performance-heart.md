YOU LIFT REGULARLY, hit your protein target, and even manage to keep death-scrolling in check. But how much attention are you giving to the muscle that powers it all? The fist-size pump that beats around 115,000 times a day to allow everything from meeting your fitness goals to digesting your food to figuring out how to break loose from your cell phone contract.

The answer, for most of us: not a lot. Heart health has historically been played as a reactive game—an annual physical, then a gentle reminder about the virtues of cardio and leafy greens, and a fix, maybe a pill, if the doc thinks we’re at risk of being one of the nearly 2,500 people in the U.S. who die of cardiovascular disease every day. But the game has evolved.

New science and revolutionary tech mean we’re able to be more engaged than ever in building a heart that lets us push the last lap, thrill to an action film, crush the day, and survive a kids’ birthday party. Metrics like VO2 max and recovery scores become something to beat. “Yes, wearables catalyzed that shift, but the bigger force is cultural. We’ve reframed heart health from disease avoidance to self-improvement, and that’s far more motivating,” says cardiologist Asim Cheema, MD, of Southlake Health in Ontario. Building a high-performance heart means keeping this vital muscle young, making it more resilient, and giving it some extra love, whether that involves refining workouts, getting real on resting, or using innovative meds. A powered-up heart can help you live longer, sure. But day to day, it’s a force multiplier that enables your energy-driven life. Use these strategies to build yours and keep it strong.
