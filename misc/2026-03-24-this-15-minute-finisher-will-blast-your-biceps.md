Start with 10 to 12 lying cable curls. Work to keep your upper arms nearly parallel to the floor here. If they start to rise, that's a sign that your front shoulders are doing some of the work. You're doing this workout for biceps, so front shoulder involvement isn't something you want.

As soon as you finish those, immediately get into a squat position with your upper legs almost parallel to the floor. Rest your arms just above the elbow right on your knees, pull your shoulders back just slightly, and curl, shooting for 10 to 12 reps. The motion and angle of pull here is similar to what you'd get if you were doing a preacher curl.

Finish those and then stand up and curl until you can't do any more with clean form. Again, try to avoid letting your elbows rise in front of you.

Do this workout with light loads for a little extra bicep work at the end of a heavy back day, on its own with heavier weights on an arm day, or do it right before date night, for a little extra arm pop in your T-shirt.
