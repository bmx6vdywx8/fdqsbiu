Understanding Gut Barrier Function

Your gut barrier is a multi-layered defense system that:

Physical barrier: Tight junctions between intestinal cells prevent "leaky gut."

Chemical barrier: Mucus and antimicrobial peptides neutralize pathogens.

Immune barrier: Gut-associated lymphoid tissue (GALT) identifies and fights invaders.

When this barrier is compromised, you may experience:

Increased intestinal permeability

Chronic, low-grade inflammation

Digestive discomfort and nutrient malabsorption

Maintaining a robust gut barrier is essential for overall health, from digestion to immune resilience.
