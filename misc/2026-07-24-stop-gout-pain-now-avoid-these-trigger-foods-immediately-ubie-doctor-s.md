What foods should I avoid if I have gout?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Foods high in purines should be avoided if you have gout. Purines break down into uric acid in the body, and when uric acid crystals accumulate in your joints, they can trigger painful gout flares. Limiting purine-rich foods—such as red meat, organ meats, shellfish, and alcohol—helps keep uric acid levels in check and reduces your risk of flare-ups.

Gout is a disease where a high level of uric acid in the blood persists leading to the formation of crystals in the joints. Red meats (beef, lamb, pork)

Organ meats (liver, kidneys, heart)

Seafoods

Alcohol (especially beer and spirits)

Sugary drinks
